# LinkedIn Content Learning

*Last updated: 2026-06-24*

## Operating Defaults
- Cadence: 3 draft batches per week.
- Audience: SEO agency owners first.
- Voice: founder/operator, candid and practical.
- CTA: ask readers to comment `beta`.
- Publishing mode: drafts only; never publish automatically.
- Product stage: early-access/beta tester recruitment.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent customer counts, testimonials, performance metrics, ROI, retention, or usage stats.
- Do not imply fully automated LinkedIn publishing.
- Do not promise guaranteed SEO outcomes or rankings.
- Prefer real app capabilities visible in the repo: agency workspace, deliverables, task management, campaign planning, reporting, integrations, Basecamp sync, timer, and beta/waitlist positioning.
- Keep each post useful even before the CTA.

## Content Pillars
| Pillar | Core Angle | Product Tie-In |
|--------|------------|----------------|
| Agency delivery chaos | SEO work is scattered across PM tools, sheets, reports, and notes | Central workspace, tasks, deliverables, activity |
| Client reporting pain | Clients ask what happened and why it matters | Reports, metrics, deliverables, activity trail |
| Fulfillment visibility | Agencies need to see promised work before it becomes a client issue | Deliverables tracker, commitments, at-risk views |
| Campaign planning | Strategy should become tasks, phases, and commitments | Campaign planning, onboarding import, phase task generation |
| Building in public | Early testers can shape the product around real agency workflows | Beta/early-access recruitment |

## Hook Bank
- "The hardest part of SEO project management is not the task list."
- "Most SEO agencies do not have a reporting problem. They have an operations visibility problem."
- "If a client asks 'what did you do this month?', your PM tool probably already failed you."
- "Spreadsheets are great until every client has a different version of the truth."
- "I am looking for a few SEO agency owners to pressure-test something we are building."

## Voice Notes
- Write like a builder talking to peers, not a SaaS brand shouting benefits.
- Use concrete agency scenarios over abstract productivity claims.
- Be transparent about beta status.
- Keep the ask simple and low-friction.

## CTA Notes
- Default CTA: "Comment `beta` and I will send details."
- Alternate CTA for higher-intent posts: "Comment `beta` if you run SEO delivery for clients and want to test it."
- Avoid asking for too many actions in one post.

## Visual Ideas
- Screenshot of the workspace client list or active client view.
- Deliverables tracker showing status and risk.
- Campaign plan screen with phases/tasks/commitments.
- Report builder or dashboard view.
- Before/after diagram of spreadsheet + PM tool + reporting deck becoming one SEO PM workspace.
