# SEO Ops Center

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Web. Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript, Supabase, and Vercel.

## Users

Agency staff manage SEO delivery, client plans, reports, and approvals. Invited client contacts use a private portal after a magic link.

## Product Purpose

Help clients understand what shipped, what happens next, and what needs their input between monthly reports, especially in the first 90 days. The client portal supports retention by making actual campaign progress visible.

## Operating Context

Staff explicitly share an SEO plan and published reports with each client. Clients read progress, approve or request changes, review content, and send short notes to their account team. Staff manage invitations and respond through the workspace’s portal tab.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Client access is scoped to live contacts, their organization, and their client. Magic-link authentication and sign out are required. Internal notes, drafts, staff assignments, and timesheets do not belong in the client portal. Plan decisions are light actions, not full editing. Reporting uses existing report blocks and browser print-to-PDF.

## Brand Commitments

The client portal uses the agency’s organization name and the app’s existing default theme colors. The mockup guides layout only; it does not define the product palette or background imagery. Portal surfaces, typography colors, actions, and charts inherit semantic theme tokens and follow light/dark mode.

## Evidence on Hand

The client portal v1 brief, two equivalent dashboard references, and the existing portal implementation.

## Open Decisions

First-90-day milestones currently use dated activities in the shared SEO plan, grouped into 30-day windows from the client’s launch date. A preference question was offered; this is the working default until the user specifies another approach.
